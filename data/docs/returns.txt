# Returns and refunds

## Return window
You can return an appliance within 30 days of delivery. The unit must be complete with all of its original parts.

## Restocking fee
An opened unit that works is charged a 15% restocking fee. A defective unit has no restocking fee.

## Refunds
Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 business days of Tidewater receiving the unit. Any refund above $500 needs a manager to approve it.
