# Delivery

## Delivery window
Every delivery has a 4 hour window. The driver calls you 30 minutes before arrival.

## Rescheduling
You can reschedule up to 24 hours before the window at no charge. A change inside 24 hours costs a 49 dollar fee.

## Haul away
Tidewater can take away your old appliance for 35 dollars when it delivers the new one.
