# Dishwasher help

## Water will not drain
Error code E4 means the dishwasher cannot drain. Check the filter at the bottom of the tub for food, then check the drain hose for a kink. Run a short rinse to confirm the water leaves.

## Cleaning the filter
Twist the filter a quarter turn and lift it out. Rinse it under the tap once a month and lock it back in place.

## Cloudy glasses
Cloudy glasses usually come from hard water. Fill the rinse aid dispenser and run the descale cycle once a quarter.

## Dishes will not dry
The dishwasher dries with residual heat. Turn on the heated dry option and leave the door closed for 20 minutes after the cycle ends.
