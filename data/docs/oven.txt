# Oven help

## Self clean
The self clean cycle locks the door for 3 hours. Take out the racks and wipe up spills before you start it.

## Error F3
Error F3 means the temperature sensor is faulty. Turn the oven off at the breaker for 5 minutes and try again. If F3 returns, book a service visit.

## Gas smell
If you smell gas, leave the house and call your gas utility from outside. Do not use switches or phones inside.

## Oven power
Electric ranges need a 240 volt outlet on its own circuit. Gas ovens need a 120 volt outlet for the igniter.
