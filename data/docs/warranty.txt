# Warranty

## Coverage
Every Tidewater appliance carries a 12 month warranty on parts and labor, counted from the delivery date. Compressors and motors carry a 5 year warranty on parts only.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover cosmetic damage such as dents, scratches or a cracked door panel. It also does not cover commercial use or damage from misuse or a bad installation.

## Making a claim
Call support with the serial number and the proof of delivery. A technician visit is booked within 5 business days of the claim.
